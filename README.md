### ⚡ About me
- I design analog and mixed-signal circuits, and I'm finishing my Licenciatura in Electronic Engineering at Universidad Técnica Nacional.
- I create **Silicon Art**, short visual explanations of transistors, circuits and chips.
- I like turning theory into things you can simulate: SPICE, Python and open-source PDKs.
